A coffee pass is a prepaid bundle of cups — "30 cups, valid 30 days" — that a customer buys once and draws down over several visits.

<Info>Payment happens at your counter, not through petakopi.my. The customer pays you in cash, by transfer, or however you normally take money; you then issue the pass in the app. Nothing is charged online.</Info>

## How it works

1. You create a **pass** once — the template that says how many cups and how long it stays valid.
2. A customer pays at the counter, and you **issue** them a pass by scanning their QR.
3. On each visit you **scan their pass** and deduct the cups they used.

Steps 1 and 2 are separate on purpose. One pass can be issued to as many customers as you like, over and over.

## Creating a pass

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Coffee Passes">
    In the app, tap **Home**, then **Manage**, pick your shop, and tap **Coffee Passes**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tap New">
    Fill in the details:
    - **Name** — e.g. "Monthly 30 Cups"
    - **Cups** — How many cups the pass includes (1–100)
    - **Valid for** — How many days the pass stays usable after it is issued
    - **Terms & conditions** — What the pass covers and any rules, e.g. "Limited to Americano"
    - **Card appearance** — Pick a colour and brightness for the pass card the customer sees
    - **Active** — Toggle on to make the pass issuable
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    The pass now appears in your list and can be issued at the counter.
  </Step>
</Steps>

You can run several passes at once — a 10-cup starter and a 50-cup regular, for example.

## Issuing a pass to a customer

<Steps>
  <Step title="Take payment at the counter">
    Cash, transfer, or whatever you normally accept. petakopi.my does not handle the money.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Ask the customer to open My QR">
    They tap their profile card on the app **Home** screen to show their personal QR.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Scan it">
    On your **Home** screen, tap **Scan** and point the camera at their QR.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick the pass">
    Your active passes appear. Tap the one they paid for, and the pass is issued immediately.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The customer sees a confirmation on their own screen within a few seconds. Their QR expires after ten minutes, so if the scan fails, ask them to reopen it.

**Issued the wrong one?** Tap **Cancel** on the confirmation screen straight after issuing. You can also cancel later from the pass detail.

## Redeeming cups

<Steps>
  <Step title="Customer shows their pass">
    They open their passes in the app and tap the card to flip it and reveal the QR.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Scan and deduct">
    Tap **Scan** on your Home screen, scan their pass QR, then choose how many cups to deduct (1–5) and confirm.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Their remaining count updates while the QR is still on screen, so both of you can see it drop. Every deduction is recorded with who performed it — tap any issued pass to see its full history.

## Pass statuses

Open a pass and tap it to see everyone you have issued it to, each with a status:

| Status | What it means |
|--------|---------------|
| Active | Cups remaining and not past its expiry date |
| Used up | All cups redeemed |
| Expired | Past its validity period, with cups possibly unused |
| Cancelled | You cancelled it; can be reactivated |

Expiry is applied automatically — a pass flips to expired once its validity period is over, without you doing anything.

## Editing a pass

Most details stay editable after customers hold passes, but one does not:

| Field | Editable? | Notes |
|-------|-----------|-------|
| Name | Yes | Applies to passes already issued |
| Terms & conditions | Yes | Applies to passes already issued |
| Valid for (days) | Yes | Only affects passes issued from now on |
| Card appearance | Yes | Applies to passes already issued |
| Active | Yes | Only controls whether you can issue new ones |
| Cups | No | Changing it would alter what existing customers already paid for |

To change the number of cups, create a new pass and switch off the old one. A pass that has been issued to anyone cannot be deleted, so switching it off is how you retire it.

## Cancelling and reactivating

Cancel an issued pass from its detail screen — useful for a mis-scan, a refund, or a customer who asks to stop. Cancelling does not delete anything: the pass and its history stay, and you can reactivate it later if the customer comes back.

## Common questions

**Does the pass go into Apple Wallet or Google Wallet?**
No. Wallet passes are built around stamp cards of about ten stamps, which does not suit a 30- or 50-cup pass. Coffee passes live in the app instead, where the remaining count is always accurate.

**Can a customer use their pass at another shop?**
No. A pass belongs to the shop that issued it.

**What if the customer's phone is flat?**
Both the customer's QR and the pass QR need their phone. Nothing can be redeemed without it, which is what keeps someone else from spending their cups.

**Can I see how many cups are left across all my customers?**
Open the pass and look at the issued list — each entry shows the remaining count out of the total.
