Help customers find your shop faster by displaying a QR code at your counter. Customers scan to get the loyalty card — no need to type your shop's URL.

## How to get your poster

Each QR is unique to your shop. You can download the poster yourself:

1. Open **Manage** and choose your shop.
2. Go to **Loyalty Programs**.
3. Find the **Counter QR poster** card and tap **Download A4 poster**.
4. Print the PDF at actual size and display it at your counter.

Want a custom design? [WhatsApp us](/getting-started/contact) and we'll help.

## How customers use it

1. Display the printed QR code at your counter.
2. Customer scans it with their phone camera.
3. They land on your shop's page and tap **"Get Loyalty Card"**.
4. The card saves to Apple Wallet or Google Wallet — they're enrolled.

<Tip>**Customers don't need to download the petakopi.my app** to scan or use the loyalty card. They use any web browser, which keeps the queue moving at the counter.</Tip>
