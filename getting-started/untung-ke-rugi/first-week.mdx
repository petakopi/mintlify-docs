**Outcome:** you know which drinks make money, and the money left so far this month, estimated.

<Info>Finished [step 1](/getting-started/untung-ke-rugi/ten-minutes)? This step takes about a minute an evening.</Info>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Find what still needs a cost">
    Open **Products**. The **No cost yet (N)** chip lists every product without a cost. Start with your five best sellers. (**Needs cost (N)** is narrower: products already sold without a cost.)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Cost each one">
    Open each product's **Costing** tab, enter its **Unit cost** and save. A costed product also repairs this month's earlier sales of it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Key in today's total">
    Each evening, open **Performance → Sales activity**, tap **Add a day's total** and type **Sales from your other register**: the total on your end-of-day slip. Tap **Save total**. The first saved day sets your daily report time to midnight; change it in **Settings** if you close later.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read the money left">
    **Performance → Overview** shows **RM X left after known costs**, badged **Estimated**. In Menu Pricing, the **To raise** chip lists drinks that lose money at today's price; **You keep** shows what each one keeps.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Keyed-in days count in net sales and show as **Keyed in**. Their ingredient cost is estimated from the share your costed products use. Orders through petakopi's POS or online store are counted as **Real** on their own.
