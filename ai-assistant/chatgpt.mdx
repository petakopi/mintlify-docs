**Outcome:** ChatGPT can read your shop's reports and answer questions about them.

ChatGPT adds outside connections like petakopi.my through **Developer mode**. Which plans have it, and where its switch sits, change from time to time; if a step below doesn't match your screen, search ChatGPT's help for "developer mode".

## Connect

1. In ChatGPT on the web, open **Settings** and switch on **Developer mode**.
2. Create a new app (OpenAI also calls it a connector) and paste this URL:
   ```text
   https://petakopi.my/mcp
   ```
3. Name it `petakopi.my` and choose **OAuth** as the sign-in method, if asked.
4. Save. A petakopi.my page opens. Sign in if asked.
5. Check the shops and the list of what ChatGPT can see, then tap **Approve**.
6. In a new chat, add petakopi.my from the tools menu and ask a question such as "How did my shop do last month?"

Custom apps work in ChatGPT on the web. They may not appear in the ChatGPT phone apps.

## What ChatGPT gets

ChatGPT gets the same eight reports as Claude. It does not get the [guided prompts](/ai-assistant/claude#guided-prompts), so ask in your own words, for example:

- "Review last month's profit and give me three actions with the RM effect."
- "Which items should I raise, and to what?"
- "Which weekdays and hours fall short of break-even?"

It never sees customer names, phone numbers, order notes or feedback text, and it cannot change anything. See [what is shared](/ai-assistant/overview#what-is-shared) and the [limits](/ai-assistant/overview#limits).

## Disconnect ChatGPT

In petakopi.my, open **Manage → AI Apps** and tap **Revoke** beside the app. ChatGPT loses access immediately. You can also delete the app in ChatGPT's settings.
