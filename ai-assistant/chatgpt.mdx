**Outcome:** ChatGPT can read your shop's reports and answer questions about them.

<Info>You need a petakopi.my account that owns at least one shop, and ChatGPT open in a web browser at [chatgpt.com](https://chatgpt.com). You add petakopi.my on the web.</Info>
{/* source: https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/guides/custom-mcp-server */}

ChatGPT calls outside connections like petakopi.my **plugins**. Custom ones work on Business, Enterprise and Edu workspaces. Pro accounts can connect custom servers that only read data, which is all petakopi.my does.
{/* source: https://help.openai.com/en/articles/12584461-connecting-custom-mcp-servers-in-chatgpt (page blocks automated fetches; checked via search excerpt 2026-10-28) */}
{/* TODO: check help.openai.com/en/articles/12584461 in a browser: can Plus accounts add a custom MCP server, and does Pro still need Settings → Apps → Advanced settings → Developer mode switched on first? */}

On a **Business** workspace, only admins and owners can add a custom plugin. Ask yours to follow the steps below and publish petakopi.my to the workspace. Each member then installs it and signs in with their own petakopi.my account.
{/* source: https://help.openai.com/en/articles/12584461-connecting-custom-mcp-servers-in-chatgpt */}

## Connect

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Plugins">
    Go to [chatgpt.com/plugins](https://chatgpt.com/plugins).
    {/* source: https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/guides/custom-mcp-server */}
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a custom MCP server">
    Click the **+** button, then **Add custom MCP server**. If you don't see it, turn on **Developer mode** under **Settings → Apps → Advanced settings** and try again.
    {/* source: https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/guides/custom-mcp-server (Add custom MCP server); Developer mode path from the older developers.openai.com/api/docs/guides/developer-mode page, now 404, see the TODO above */}
  </Step>
  <Step title="Name it and paste the address">
    Type `petakopi.my` as the name. Under **Connection**, paste this as the **Server URL**:

    ```text
    https://petakopi.my/mcp
    ```
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose OAuth">
    For authentication, choose **OAuth**. Leave the client ID and secret empty: ChatGPT registers itself with petakopi.my.
    {/* source: https://developers.openai.com/plugins/build/auth */}
    {/* TODO: check the exact label ChatGPT shows when it asks how to register the OAuth client (DCR vs CIMD); petakopi.my supports dynamic client registration only */}
  </Step>
  <Step title="Accept the warning">
    Read the risk warning and select **I understand and want to continue**. Then select **Create as a plugin**.
    {/* source: https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/guides/custom-mcp-server */}
  </Step>
  <Step title="Install it and sign in">
    Install the plugin. A petakopi.my page opens. Sign in if asked.
    {/* source: https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/guides/custom-mcp-server */}
  </Step>
  <Step title="Approve on petakopi.my">
    The page says the app "wants read-only access to your petakopi.my shops". It lists **Shops it can read** and **What it can see**. Check that your shops are there, then tap **Approve**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Ask your first question

Start a new chat. Type `@` in the message box and pick **petakopi.my**, then ask:
{/* source: https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/guides/custom-mcp-server */}

```text
@petakopi.my How did we do this month?
```

## Ready-made questions

The three guided prompts are a Claude feature. In ChatGPT, paste one of these instead:

- "Review last month's profit and give me three actions with the RM effect of each."
- "Which items should I raise, and to what?"
- "Plan next month: what do I need to sell a day, and which weekdays and hours fall short of break-even?"

ChatGPT reads the same eight reports as Claude. It never sees customer names, phone numbers, order notes or feedback text, and it cannot change anything. See [what is shared](/ai-assistant/overview#what-is-shared) and the [limits](/ai-assistant/overview#limits).

## Disconnect ChatGPT

Open **Manage** for your shop, tap **AI Apps** in the sidebar, find ChatGPT and tap **Revoke**. ChatGPT loses access straight away. To use it again, install the plugin and approve it again.

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="&quot;Couldn't connect&quot; or a 404 error">
    Check the address is exactly `https://petakopi.my/mcp`. If it is, Ask AI isn't switched on for your account yet. Message us on WhatsApp and we will turn it on.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="petakopi.my says the account doesn't own a coffee shop">
    You signed in with an account that doesn't own a shop. Sign out of petakopi.my in that browser, connect again, and sign in with the account you use in Manage.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="ChatGPT says it is signed out or the token expired">
    Connect again: open the plugin at [chatgpt.com/plugins](https://chatgpt.com/plugins), sign in again when asked, and tap **Approve** on petakopi.my.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="One of my shops is missing">
    ChatGPT only sees shops your account owns. If a shop is missing from **Shops it can read**, sign in with the account that owns that shop.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="ChatGPT answers without your numbers">
    Start a new chat, type `@` and pick **petakopi.my** before your question.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
