**Outcome:** you ask an AI assistant "Did we make money last month?" and it answers from your own petakopi.my numbers.

petakopi.my has a read-only connection for AI assistants. You sign in once on petakopi.my, approve the connection, and the assistant can then read reports for the shops you own. It cannot change prices, send messages or edit anything.

## What you can ask

The assistant can read eight reports. You don't need to name them; ask your question and it picks the right ones.

| Report | Ask things like |
| --- | --- |
| Your shops | "Which of my shops have profit reports ready?" |
| Sales summary | "How did we do this week compared with last week?" |
| Sales by day, weekday or hour | "Which hours are dead on weekdays?" |
| Menu items, prices and margins | "Which drinks should I raise, and to what?" |
| Break-even and pricing inputs | "How much do I need to sell a day to cover costs?" |
| Monthly profit and loss | "Did we cover our costs in September?" |
| Monthly expenses by category | "Why were costs higher this month?" |
| Loyalty and voucher health | "Did the Merdeka voucher pay for itself?" |

The answers are only as complete as your setup:

- **Profit and loss** needs a Daily report time. Set one on [Performance](/manage-shop/performance/overview). Sales reports work without it.
- **Menu margins** need item costs. Items without a cost show as "Add a cost". See [Price your menu](/manage-shop/performance/menu-pricing).
- **Loyalty** needs a loyalty programme. See [Loyalty card for owners](/loyalty-card/for-owners).

If something is missing, the assistant tells you what to set up and links to the page.

## What is shared

When you approve the connection, petakopi.my shows the shops the assistant can read and the kinds of data it can see. It can read:

- Your shops and how they are set up
- Sales totals by period, channel and payment method
- Sales by day, weekday and hour
- Menu items, prices, ingredient costs and margins
- Break-even and pricing inputs
- Monthly profit and loss
- Monthly expenses by category
- Loyalty stamps, rewards and voucher use

It never sees customer names, phone numbers, order notes or feedback text. It only sees shops you own.

## Privacy

The reports go to the AI provider you connected, such as Anthropic or OpenAI, and are handled under that provider's terms and your settings there. petakopi.my logs which report was requested and when, so you can see when an app was last used.

## Disconnect an app

1. Open **Manage** for your shop.
2. In the sidebar, tap **AI Apps**.
3. Find the app in **Connected AI apps** and tap **Revoke**.

The app is disconnected immediately. To use it again, connect it and approve it again. You can also remove the connector inside Claude or ChatGPT.

## Limits

- **Read-only.** The assistant can read reports but cannot change anything. Make changes in [Manage](https://petakopi.my/manage).
- **Up to 5 minutes old.** Figures can lag behind the Manage pages by a few minutes.
- **Loyverse items.** Loyverse receipts count in takings but not in item units, so menu reports count only petakopi.my online and POS orders.
- **Takings and net sales differ.** Sales reports count an order when it is paid. Profit and loss uses Performance net sales, so the two can differ slightly.
- **Check the numbers.** An assistant can misread a table or make a sum wrong. Before acting on a big decision, check the figure on the matching Manage page.
