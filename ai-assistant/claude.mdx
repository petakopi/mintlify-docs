**Outcome:** Claude can read your shop's reports and answer questions about them.

<Info>You need a petakopi.my account that owns at least one shop. Keep your petakopi.my email and password handy: you sign in once during setup.</Info>

## Connect

Pick where you use Claude. Most owners pick **claude.ai and Desktop**.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="claude.ai and Desktop">
    Custom connectors work on the Free, Pro, Max, Team and Enterprise plans. The Free plan allows one custom connector.
    {/* source: https://claude.com/docs/connectors/custom/add-unlisted */}

    <Steps>
      <Step title="Switch on AI access">
        Open **Manage → AI Apps** for the shop and switch on AI access. See [Switch on AI access for the shop](/ai-assistant/overview#switch-on-ai-access-for-the-shop).
      </Step>
      <Step title="Open Connectors">
        On [claude.ai](https://claude.ai) or in Claude Desktop, open **Customize**, then **Connectors**.
        {/* source: https://claude.com/docs/connectors/getting-started */}
      </Step>
      <Step title="Add a custom connector">
        Click **+ Add**, then **Add custom connector**.
        {/* source: https://support.claude.com/en/articles/11175166-getting-started-with-custom-connectors-using-remote-mcp */}
      </Step>
      <Step title="Paste the petakopi.my address">
        Type `petakopi.my` as the name and paste this address as the URL:

        ```text
        https://petakopi.my/mcp
        ```

        Click **Continue**.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Keep the sign-in settings">
        Claude fills in the sign-in settings for you. Keep them, click **Continue**, then click **Add**. If Claude asks how to register its OAuth client, choose **Register automatically**.
        {/* source: https://claude.com/docs/connectors/custom/add-unlisted#choose-authentication-settings */}
      </Step>
      <Step title="Connect and sign in">
        Click **Connect**. A petakopi.my page opens: in the same tab on claude.ai, or in your browser if you use Claude Desktop. Sign in if asked.
        {/* source: https://claude.com/docs/connectors/getting-started */}
      </Step>
      <Step title="Approve on petakopi.my">
        The page says the app "wants read-only access to your petakopi.my shops". It lists **Shops it can read** and **What it can see**. Check that your shops are there, then tap **Approve**.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Check it is connected">
        Back in Claude, petakopi.my shows under **Your connectors** as **Connected**.
        {/* source: https://claude.com/docs/connectors/getting-started */}
      </Step>
    </Steps>

        {/* source: https://claude.com/docs/plugins/overview#bundled-connectors */}
      </Step>

      </Step>

## Ask your first question

On claude.ai or Desktop, start a new chat. Click **+** at the bottom left of the message box, open **Connectors** and check that petakopi.my is switched on.
{/* source: https://claude.com/docs/connectors/getting-started */}

Then ask:

```text
How did we do this month?
```

Claude may ask before it runs a report. Click **Always allow** so it stops asking for that report.
{/* source: https://claude.com/docs/connectors/getting-started */}

## Guided prompts

petakopi.my comes with three ready-made prompts. Each one runs the right reports in order and gives a short answer with a table. You can add a shop and a month; leave them out and Claude uses your only shop and the most recent month.

| Prompt | What you get |
| --- | --- |
| Monthly profit review | The month's verdict in RM, the three biggest changes from the month before, anything missing, and three actions for next month |
| Find pricing opportunities | The five best price changes, with the monthly RM effect of each, and the best sellers that still need a cost |
| Plan next month | A daily sales target, the weekdays and hours that fall short, and a weekly plan that allows for Malaysian holidays and paydays |

**On claude.ai and Desktop:** click **+** in the message box, open **Connectors**, point at **petakopi.my**, and pick a prompt.
{/* source: https://modelcontextprotocol.io/docs/develop/connect-remote-servers */}

**In Claude Code:** type `/` and look for the three prompts under petakopi. You can also type them in full:
{/* source: https://code.claude.com/docs/en/mcp */}

```text
/mcp__petakopi__monthly_profit_review
/mcp__petakopi__pricing_opportunities
/mcp__petakopi__plan_next_month
```

These full names are for the `claude mcp add` connection above. If you installed the plugin, pick the prompts from the `/` list instead.

## What Claude can see

Claude reads the same eight reports on every surface. It never sees customer names, phone numbers, order notes or feedback text, and it cannot change anything. See [what is shared](/ai-assistant/overview#what-is-shared) and the [limits](/ai-assistant/overview#limits).

## Disconnect Claude

<Steps>
  <Step title="Revoke on petakopi.my">
    Open **Manage** for your shop, tap **AI Apps** in the sidebar, find Claude and tap **Revoke**. Claude loses access straight away.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Remove it from Claude (optional)">
    On claude.ai or Desktop, open **Customize → Connectors**, select petakopi.my, open the three-dot menu and select **Remove**. In Claude Code, run `claude mcp remove petakopi`.
    {/* source: https://claude.com/docs/connectors/getting-started#manage-or-disconnect-a-connector */}
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="&quot;AI access is off for &lt;shop&gt;&quot;">
    AI access is switched off for that shop. Open **Manage → AI Apps** for the shop, switch on AI access, then ask again. You don't need to connect again.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="The approval page says no shop is switched on">
    None of your shops has AI access switched on yet. Open **Manage → AI Apps**, switch on AI access for a shop, then connect again.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="&quot;Couldn't connect&quot; or a 404 error">
    Check the address is exactly `https://petakopi.my/mcp`. If it is, Ask AI isn't switched on for your account yet. Message us on WhatsApp and we will turn it on.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="petakopi.my says the account doesn't own a coffee shop">
    You signed in with an account that doesn't own a shop. Sign out of petakopi.my in that browser, connect again, and sign in with the account you use in Manage.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Claude says it is signed out or the token expired">
    Connect again. On claude.ai or Desktop, open **Customize → Connectors**, select petakopi.my, click **Disconnect**, then **Connect** and approve. In Claude Code, type `/mcp`, select petakopi and choose **Re-authenticate**.
    {/* source: https://claude.com/docs/connectors/getting-started#manage-or-disconnect-a-connector */}
    {/* source: https://code.claude.com/docs/en/mcp */}
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="One of my shops is missing">
    Claude only sees shops your account owns and has switched on. If a shop is missing from **Shops it can read**, switch on AI access for it under **Manage → AI Apps**. If it is still missing, sign in with the account that owns that shop.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Claude doesn't use petakopi.my">
    Click **+** in the message box, open **Connectors** and switch petakopi.my on for this chat. Then name it in your question, for example "Using petakopi.my, how did we do this month?"
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
