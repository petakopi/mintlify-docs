**Outcome:** Claude can read your shop's reports and answer questions about them.

You need a petakopi.my account that owns at least one shop. Pick where you use Claude:

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="claude.ai and Desktop">
    Add petakopi.my as a custom connector. Connectors you add on claude.ai also appear in Claude Desktop.

    1. In Claude, open **Customize → Connectors**.
    2. Click **+ Add**, then **Add custom connector**.
    3. Name it `petakopi.my` and paste this URL, then click **Continue**:
       ```text
       https://petakopi.my/mcp
       ```
    4. Keep the sign-in settings Claude detects, click **Continue**, then click **Add**.
    5. Click **Connect**. A petakopi.my page opens. Sign in if asked.
    6. Check the shops and the list of what Claude can see, then tap **Approve**.

    To use it in a chat, click **+** at the bottom left of the message box, open **Connectors** and switch petakopi.my on.

    On a Team or Enterprise plan, an Owner adds the connector once in **Organization settings → Connectors**. Each member then finds it in **Customize → Connectors** and clicks **Connect** to sign in with their own petakopi.my account.

## Guided prompts

petakopi.my comes with three ready-made prompts. Each one runs the right reports in order and gives a short answer with a table. You can add a shop and a month; leave them out and Claude uses your only shop and the most recent month.

| Prompt | What you get |
| --- | --- |
| Monthly profit review | The month's verdict in RM, the three biggest changes from the month before, anything missing, and three actions for next month |
| Find pricing opportunities | The five best price changes, with the monthly RM effect of each, and the best sellers that still need a cost |
| Plan next month | A daily sales target, the weekdays and hours that fall short, and a weekly plan that allows for Malaysian holidays and paydays |

In Claude Code, type them as slash commands:

```text
/mcp__petakopi__monthly_profit_review
/mcp__petakopi__pricing_opportunities
/mcp__petakopi__plan_next_month
```

These names are for the `claude mcp add` connection above. If you installed the plugin, type `/` and look for the same three names under petakopi.

On claude.ai and Desktop, open the **+** menu in the message box, choose petakopi.my, and pick a prompt.

## What Claude can see

Claude reads the same eight reports on every surface. It never sees customer names, phone numbers, order notes or feedback text, and it cannot change anything. See [what is shared](/ai-assistant/overview#what-is-shared) and the [limits](/ai-assistant/overview#limits).

## Disconnect Claude

In petakopi.my, open **Manage → AI Apps** and tap **Revoke** beside the app. Claude loses access immediately. In Claude, you can also remove the connector from **Customize → Connectors**, or run `claude mcp remove petakopi` in Claude Code.
