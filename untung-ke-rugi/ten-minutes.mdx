**Outcome:** you know the sales a day that covers your rent, wages and bills, and what one drink keeps.

<Info>You need a claimed shop first: see [Claim your coffee shop](/getting-started/claim-ownership). No orders, no bills and no POS are needed for this step.</Info>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Menu Pricing">
    Sign in at [petakopi.my/manage](https://petakopi.my/manage), pick your shop, then open **Costs & Pricing → Menu Pricing**. The first visit walks you through four short steps.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Type what you pay each month">
    In **Operations**, enter **Rent, wages and bills** for a month. Your best guess is fine; it shows as **Estimated** until a bill replaces it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Type what you sell">
    Enter **Sales a day** and **Days open a month** (26 is a typical month). Not sure of the daily total? Tap **Add it up item by item**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Cost one drink">
    Open **Products**, pick your best seller, open its **Costing** tab and enter the **Unit cost** of its beans, milk and cup. Save. A full recipe can wait.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read the two figures">
    Back in Menu Pricing, under the bar: "About RM X in sales a day covers rent, wages and bills; about RM Y in sales a day also keeps your N%." The costed drink shows **You keep RM Z**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Worked example: rent, wages and bills RM 3,200.00, 30 cups a day at RM 8.86, 26 days open. The page says about RM 220.00 a day covers the bills and about RM 270.00 a day also keeps 10%.
