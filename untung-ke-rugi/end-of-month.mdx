**Outcome:** Costs covered, or Shortfall, with the real numbers behind it.

<Info>Finished [step 2](/untung-ke-rugi/first-week)? Set aside about half an hour at month end.</Info>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Snap each bill">
    In the petakopi.my app, open your shop's **Expenses** and add a photo of each bill: rent, electricity, water, the wages slip. Each one appears on the web as a **Draft**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm on the web">
    Open **Performance → Expenses → Invoices**. Open each draft, check the supplier, date and total, choose what it is for, then confirm. When you estimated the same category for that month, the invoice asks whether it is **that cost**: say yes and the real amount replaces the estimate.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Answer the Monthly review">
    Open **Performance → Monthly review**. Each row asks one plain question: are all sales in, were wages reviewed, is each cost confirmed or zero, is every sold item costed. A cost carried from last month asks whether it is right for this month: confirm it, or choose **Change in Expenses**. Tap **Save answers**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read the verdict">
    The top of the page reads **Costs covered** or **Shortfall**. Every figure carries **Real**, **Estimated** or **Mixed**, so you can see what is still a guess. Overview shows the same verdict for the month.
  </Step>
</Steps>
