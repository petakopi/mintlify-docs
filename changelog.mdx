Stay up to date with the latest changes to petakopi.my.

<Update label="2026-08-01" description="v0.10.0" tags={["Mobile"]}>
### New

- **Coffee Passes** — Buy a prepaid pass at a shop and redeem cups from your phone. Passes appear as wallet-style cards you can flip to reveal the QR, each with its own redemption history.
- **Coffee Passes for shop owners** — Create pass programs, pick the card colour, issue passes to customers, and scan to deduct a cup. A cancelled pass can be reactivated later.
- **My QR** — Your personal QR now sits on the Home profile card, so an owner can scan you to issue a pass or add a stamp.
- **Choose your theme** — Five colour themes under Profile, Appearance: Classic, Cream, Emerald, Sand, and Dusty Rose. On iPhone the app icon changes to match the theme you pick.
- **Drink name suggestions** — The Coffee Journal suggests drinks you have added before, so your regular order is one tap instead of retyping it.

### Improved

- **My Cards** — Redesigned as wallet-style cards. Tap a card to flip it and reveal its QR instead of opening a separate sheet.
- **Profile** — Grouped into cards, with a dark and light toggle you can reach without digging through settings.
- **Orders** — Clearer rows in the list, a redesigned timeline on the order detail, and a Contact button for the shop.
- **Coffee Journal photos** — A photo can keep its original background instead of being cut out into a sticker
- **Coffee Journal privacy** — The add form now tells you where your cups are visible before you add one
- **Sheets** — Shop details and QR sheets can be dragged down to dismiss
- **Map colours** — The coffee shop map follows the theme colour you have chosen
- **Coffee Journal loading** — The journal reopens instantly when you come back to it

### Fixed

- Fixed an issue where the app opened with a light background in dark mode after being closed for a while
- Fixed an issue on Android where changing the theme closed the app and returned you to the home screen
- Fixed an issue where you could not add a second cup to a day that already had one
- Fixed an issue where the journal strip on the Home screen shifted as its data loaded
- Fixed an issue where a used or cancelled pass still offered a QR code or a cancel action
- Fixed an issue where long pass terms could not be collapsed
</Update>

<Update label="2026-07-17" description="v0.9.0" tags={["Mobile"]}>
### New

- **Coffee Journal** — Log your daily cups in a month calendar with photo stickers. Link entries to a coffee shop, add photos, and see your week at a glance on the Home screen. Tap any empty day to add a cup.
- **Bought Beans** — Track the coffee bags you buy: origins with per-origin roast, processing, and altitude details, tasting notes, brew method, photos, and a spending chart
- **Orders** — Customers get a My Orders screen to follow their orders, and shop owners get an Orders tab in shop management. Tapping an order notification opens that order directly, and order lists refresh automatically when an update arrives while the app is open.
- **Train lines on the map** — Toggle transit lines and station names on the coffee shop map, with per-line controls so you only see the lines you care about
- **Place search on the explore map** — Search for a place and jump straight to it on the map
- **Custom links for your shop** — Owners can add their own links to their shop page, with icons, thumbnails, and colors, and reorder them any time
- **More bean filters** — Filter beans by weight range and find recently added beans with the new "New" filter and badge
- **Marketing message preview** — See a breakdown of who will receive a marketing message before you send it

### Improved

- **Home screen** — Quick Links and For Owners are now a cleaner action grid, with smoother loading while your data arrives
- **My Loyalty Cards** — Now a full page instead of a pop-up sheet, making cards easier to browse
- **Photo uploads** — Pick multiple photos at once, uploads run in the background so saving is faster, and failed uploads can be retried with a tap

### Fixed

- Fixed an issue where loyalty card scan errors were hidden behind the camera view
</Update>

<Update label="2026-07-02" description="v0.8.0" tags={["Mobile"]}>
### Improved

- **Better crash reporting** — Crash reports now include more accurate details across iOS and Android, so we can find and fix problems faster
- **Smarter update diagnostics** — The app now tells our servers which app version it's running, helping us spot version-specific issues and keep older versions working smoothly
</Update>

<Update label="2026-05-11" description="v0.7.4" tags={["Mobile"]}>
### New

- **Track expenses for your shop** — Log, list, edit, and delete expense entries directly from the app (previously web-only). Photos, PDFs, and document uploads are all supported.
- **Has Loyalty Card filter** — Quick filter on the discovery list to find shops that offer loyalty cards

### Improved

- **Skip shop picker for single-shop owners** — If you only manage one coffee shop, tapping Manage now jumps you straight to its dashboard. Multi-shop owners keep the picker.
- **Customer email on scan** — When scanning or tapping a customer's loyalty card, their email now shows on the card details and actions screens so you can identify repeat visitors. Falls back to "Guest" when no email is on file.
- **Active Cards stat** — The Loyalty manage tile now shows a single cross-platform number instead of the confusing "X / Y" ratio that hid Google Wallet cards
- **Gift badge on shops with loyalty** — A gift badge now appears beside coffee shop names on listings for shops that have an active loyalty program
- **Smoother form keyboards** — Forms across the app now have cleaner focus transitions and stay above the keyboard when editing fields lower on the screen

### Fixed

- Fixed the opening hours section on coffee shop pages collapsing multi-slot days into a single row, and the missing "Opens tomorrow at..." next-change text
- Fixed the keyboard covering colour pickers when editing the bottom of the Card Appearance screen
- Fixed a stuck "Loading card..." state after scanning a QR code from your photo library
</Update>

<Update label="2026-03-30" description="v0.7.1" tags={["Mobile"]}>
### New

- **Customize your loyalty card look** — Choose your own colors for how your loyalty card appears in Apple Wallet and Google Wallet, with a live preview as you design
- **Clear opening hours** — Quickly remove all opening hours from the app when your schedule changes
- **Remove a loyalty card** — Customers can now remove a loyalty card they no longer need

### Improved

- **Editing loyalty programs** — You can now edit the program name, terms & conditions, expiration days, and active status even when customers already have cards. Only the stamps required field remains locked.
- **Loyalty card list** — Card serial numbers are now easier to read with a visible prefix
- **Location on map** — Your location dot on the map is now more accurate and always visible
- **Wallet updates** — Changes to your card colors and program name now show up in Apple Wallet right away. Google Wallet updates are picked up the next time a stamp is added or a new card is issued, due to Google's update restrictions.

### Fixed

- Fixed an issue where re-adding a loyalty card on iPhone incorrectly showed a "Download cancelled" message
</Update>
