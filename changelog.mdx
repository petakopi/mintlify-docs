<Update label="2026-10-15" description="v0.16.0" tags={["Mobile"]}>
### New

- **Vouchers at checkout** — Customers can enter a voucher code when they order from your Online Store and see the discount before they pay. The discount shows on the order and its receipt.
- **Vouchers at the counter** — In POS, pick a voucher from quick chips or the full list, or type a code. A voucher sale needs a connection; sales without a voucher still work offline.
- **Open items** — Charge something that is not on your menu by entering its name and price in POS
- **Swipe to update orders** — In Orders, swipe an order to reveal the next step, such as start preparing, mark ready, complete, or confirm, then tap it

### Improved

- **POS ticket** — Plain drinks go on the ticket in one tap, repeat taps add to the same line, and menu cards show how many are on the ticket and which are sold out. Swipe a ticket line to remove it, and the total sits large above Charge.
- **Cash payments** — Pick a quick cash amount, see the change once enough cash is entered, and keep Save above the keyboard
- **POS in landscape** — Menus fit three cards per row, sheets dock to the left so the ticket stays visible, and the cart button hides the ticket for a wider menu
- **POS settings** — Payment settings is now POS settings. Choose which menu this station sells, and see progress and a confirmation when you refresh the menu. An empty menu points you to add products on the web.
- **Printing** — Print a ticket again from a sale receipt or POS history, and turn on auto print in print station settings. Counter sales are labelled as such on the ticket.
- **Expenses** — Take a photo or pick a file of a receipt to save it as a draft, then add the amount and category on the web. The tab shows how many drafts are waiting, and you can pull down to refresh.
- **Online Store** — Products, menus, and store settings now open on the web
- **Home** — The Customize tile is gone. Arrange Home under Me → Preferences → Home layout, where owners see customer and owner tiles together and can hide Inbox. Scan on the customer Home asks before switching you to Owner view.
- **DuitNow QR** — When a shop takes DuitNow QR, the payment screen tells customers to screenshot the QR and scan it from their bank app
- **Store products** — The cart count shows in a corner tab on each product, like the web store
- **Fresh data** — Screens refresh when you come back to the app
- **Beans** — Every bean you log is a bought bean. The tasted option, its date picker, and its filter are gone, and photos show on a plain background.

### Fixed

- Fixed an issue where a screen title could run into its header buttons
- Fixed an issue where content sat under the notch or home bar in landscape
- Fixed an issue where the product, options, and cash sheets jumped too far above the keyboard
- Fixed an issue where the Sync Ratings button sat against the empty text

</Update>

<Update label="2026-10-06" description="v0.15.0" tags={["Mobile"]}>
### New

- **Point of sale** — In Owner mode, charge cash or confirm a QR payment on the phone, including when the connection drops. The sale stays on the phone and uploads when you are back online, with its own receipt number.
- **Share your coffee month** — From the journal calendar, make a picture of that month's public cups and what you spent, then share it. Private cups stay off the picture.
- **Online Store for owners** — Edit products, categories, options, ordering hours, and payment methods on the phone. You can pause ordering, and customers can still read the menu.
- **Android print station** — Use this phone to print kitchen tickets and customer receipts on a paired Bluetooth printer

### Improved

- **Ordering** — Pick a pickup time inside the shop's hours, resume or replace an unpaid order, and cancel an order that has not been paid. The menu stays readable outside ordering hours, and drink photos show on the cart and at checkout.
- **Home** — Hold and drag quick actions into the order you want. Customer and owner Homes keep their own layout, saved for the email you are signed in with.
- **Coffee Journal** — A finished sticker sits on dotted paper with space around it. A photo can fill in the cup's date and time from the camera clock, and you can still change it.
- **Bought beans** — Large bag photos stay sharp, and you can open the full-size picture. The list and the spending summary count bags, weight, and what you spent the same way.
- **Shop hours** — A shop marked temporarily closed shows that on its hours card, even during its usual open hours
- **Find a customer** — Look up by username, email, or phone number, and return to the results after opening a card
- **Online payments** — Connect CHIP as well as Bayarcash when you set up FPX
- **Shop pages** — The premium badge and gold photo overlay no longer appear. Owner shops still show the green check.
- **Notifications** — Tapping a notification marks it read

### Fixed

- Fixed an issue where the selected event chip was hard to read in dark mode
- Fixed an issue where drink-name suggestions wrapped onto a second line

</Update>

- **Recently viewed** — Events and beans you opened sit on Home next to shops
- **Nearby in Community** — Switching to Nearby, or pulling to refresh, takes a fresh location if the last one is stale

### Fixed

- Fixed the tab bar showing through the Community feed while it loaded

</Update>

<Update label="2026-08-08" description="v0.12.0" tags={["Mobile"]}>
### New

- **Community** — A feed of what people are drinking and the beans they have bought. Comment, reply, and like, tap a name to see just that person's posts, and open a bean's full details without leaving the feed. First-time commenters verify a phone number once to keep spam out.
- **Order from the app** — Browse a shop's menu, build a cart, and check out without leaving the app. Pay online with FPX or settle at the counter, and your contact details carry over from your last order.
- **Sticker playground** — Tap the dice on the Coffee Journal calendar to tip the month's stickers into a box you can tilt, shake, drag, and double-tap to scatter. Switch it to shop logos if you prefer.
- **Coffee Passes for owners** — Pass programs now have their own stats and a full activity log, so you can see what was issued and redeemed.
- **Find a customer without a QR** — Look someone up by username or phone number at the counter when scanning is not practical. A typed lookup can add a stamp or deduct a cup, but never issue a new pass.
- **Make Home yours** — Hide the sections you do not use, and shop owners get a Home laid out for running a shop rather than browsing one. A dot on the Home tab tells you when something needs attention.
- **Recently viewed shops** — The shops you looked at recently sit on Home, ready to reopen or clear.
- **Add your phone number** — Verify a number from Edit profile once, then change it later if you switch SIM.
- **Stamp notifications on Android** — Get a notification when a shop stamps your card or you redeem a reward.
- **Expense receipts** — Preview a receipt inside the app, pick entry dates from a calendar, and jump straight to an expense from its notification.

### Improved

- **Coffee Journal stats** — Swipe between this month and your lifetime totals, and the figures follow whichever month you are browsing rather than always showing today's.
- **Loyalty history** — Each stamp and redemption now says who it was for and who added it, instead of only when it happened.
- **Scanning a card** — The customer's name shows the moment you scan, rather than appearing only after the first stamp.
- **Ratings** — Tap the star you already gave to clear a rating; there was previously no way to undo one.
- **Journal photos** — Page through a day's photos in the full-screen viewer instead of closing and reopening each one.
- **Loading** — The journal, community sheets, and a day's entries now show the shape of what is coming instead of a spinner, and stickers no longer sit blank while they load.
- **Sign out** — Signing out clears this device's local data, so a shared phone does not leak the previous account's history.
- **Profile** — Shows the installed build and tells you when a newer version is available.

### Fixed

- Fixed a crash on iPhone that could close the app when a switch was tapped as a screen was closing
- Fixed the app being closed by the system after playing with the sticker box and leaving it open in the background
- Fixed a crash in Community when replying to a comment for the first time
- Fixed the keyboard covering the comment box, the link editor, and the bean and shop filter forms
- Fixed a verified phone number showing as missing on Edit profile until you signed out and back in
- Fixed comment, like, and reply counts not refreshing when the feed reloaded
- Fixed tapping a new-order notification not opening that order
- Fixed thrown stickers escaping the playground box
- Fixed shop logos blending into the background on the journal calendar and week strip
- Fixed a day with more than one cup not showing its count on the Home week strip

</Update>
