title: "Recipes"
description: "Build recipes from ingredients and quantities per serving"
---

A recipe describes the ingredients and quantities used to make a serving. Your storefront product is a separate record with its own selling price and customer choices. You build it by adding lines — each line is `{Item, quantity}` — and the app sums the line costs to give you per-cup COGS.

For Performance, [link recipes to saved products and options](/manage-shop/performance/costing). This does not require Loyverse or change storefront selling prices. The Loyverse sales columns described below belong to the existing integration.

## Create a recipe

1. Open **Costs & Pricing → Recipes** and create a recipe.
2. Enter its name.
3. Add the ingredients and packaging used for one serving, with their quantities.
4. Review the ingredient cost and save the recipe.
5. [Link the recipe to your product in Costing](/manage-shop/performance/costing).

Set the selling price under **Online Store → Products**. Recipe creation and editing focus on ingredients and quantities; they do not ask for a selling price or target food-cost percentage.

## Previously saved pricing values

Older recipes may still have a target food-cost percentage or menu price saved. Editing ingredients does not clear them. These values are separate from your storefront product prices and are no longer editable in the recipe form.

## Example: Hot Coffee Latte

Using the [items defined on the Items page](/manage-shop/costs-and-pricing/items), the Hot Coffee Latte recipe has three lines:

| Item | Quantity | Per-unit cost | Line cost |
|---|---|---|---|
| Whole milk | 150 ml | RM 0.008 / ml | RM 1.20 |
| Coffee beans | 18 g | RM 0.0889 / g | RM 1.60 |
| Paper cup | 1 | RM 0.30 | RM 0.30 |
| | | **Per-cup COGS** | **RM 3.10** |

## Minimal and Full views

The Recipes index has a **Minimal / Full** toggle at the top right, next to the search box.

- **Minimal** (the default) shows just the recipe names — a clean list for finding and editing.
- **Full** adds six columns: **COGS**, **Target food cost**, **Quick price**, **Sold this month**, **Revenue**, **Gross margin**. Target food cost and Quick price reflect previously saved targets. Every column header sorts, so you can rank by margin or by what's selling.

<Warning>Everything the numbers below describe lives in **Full** view. If you've just mapped your Loyverse items and the index looks unchanged, you're in Minimal — flip the toggle. Your choice is remembered in that browser.</Warning>

The search box filters by recipe name and works in both views.

## Per-recipe sales (after Loyverse linkage)

If you map Loyverse variants to recipes (see [Loyverse Items](/manage-shop/costs-and-pricing/loyverse-items)), three of the Full-view columns come alive: **Sold this month**, **Revenue**, **Gross margin**.

Without mapping, the Recipes index still works for setup and pricing — you just don't see per-recipe sales numbers. Mapping is incremental: cover your top sellers first, leave rare items unmapped. They'll still count in your overall [Pulse](/manage-shop/pulse/overview) revenue.

## What's next

With your top recipes built and per-cup COGS computed, head to [Performance](/manage-shop/performance/overview) to record your fixed costs (rent, wages, utilities) and see whether each month actually made money.
