The **Costs & Pricing** sidebar group is where you set up your menu, calculate what each cup costs you, and decide what to charge.

<Info>This is the "what should I sell, and at what price?" half of the manage panel. The other half — checking how you actually did — lives in [Performance](/manage-shop/performance/overview).</Info>

## How P&L works (the short version)

Every coffee shop's bottom line is a chain of subtractions:

```
Revenue − COGS         = Gross profit
Gross profit − Fixed costs = Net profit
Net profit ÷ Revenue   = Net margin
```

- **Revenue** is what comes through the till.
- **COGS** (cost of goods sold) is the variable cost of what you sold — milk, beans, cups. It goes up when you sell more.
- **Fixed costs** are rent, wages, utilities, internet — they don't move when you sell more cups.
- **Net margin** is what's left, expressed as a % of revenue. Healthy small coffee shops in Malaysia aim for 10–20% net margin.

Costs & Pricing is where you fill in the inputs:

- **Items + Recipes** give you per-cup COGS.

[Performance](/manage-shop/performance/overview) is where your actual sales, expenses and profit come together each month.

## Setup walkthrough

If you're starting fresh, do these in order. Each step takes 5–15 minutes.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Add your raw materials">
    Open [Items](/manage-shop/costs-and-pricing/items). Add each ingredient you buy — beans, milk, cups, syrups. Pack size, pack price, yield %. Items power per-cup COGS.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Build your menu">
    Open [Recipes](/manage-shop/costs-and-pricing/recipes). One recipe per sellable drink. Each line is `{Item, quantity}`; the app sums to give you per-cup COGS plus a Quick price suggestion. Build your top sellers first — you don't have to map every drink on day one.
  </Step>
</Steps>

After this, head to [Performance](/manage-shop/performance/overview) to see how the month actually went.
