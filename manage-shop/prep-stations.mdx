---
title: "Prep stations"
description: "Print a separate ticket for the bar and the kitchen"
---

Prep stations let your bar and your kitchen each get their own ticket. Drinks print at **Bar**, food prints at **Kitchen**, and nobody has to read past the other station's lines.

<Info>Prep stations are rolling out shop by shop. If you don't see **Settings → Printing** in the Manage panel yet, ask the petakopi.my team to turn it on for your shop.</Info>

Every shop starts with one station called **Kitchen**. Until you add another, everything prints exactly as it does today.

## Set up Bar and Kitchen on the web

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Printing">
    In the Manage panel, go to **Settings → Printing**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a station">
    Tap **Add prep station**, name it (for example **Bar**) and tick the categories it makes, such as **Coffee** and **Non-coffee**. You can rename **Kitchen** too.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Leave the rest on Kitchen">
    Any category without a station prints at your first station, shown as **Default (Kitchen)**. Sections like **Bestsellers** can stay that way.
  </Step>
</Steps>

You can also pick a category's station when you edit the category.

### Check where a product prints

On the product form, under the categories, a short line tells you where it prints, for example **Prints at Bar · from Coffee**.

If a product sits in two categories that print at different stations, the line shows both and a **Print at** choice. Pick one so the product prints in one place. Until you do, it follows your category order, and **Settings → Printing** shows how many products still need a station.

## Claim stations on the phone

Each station prints on one Android phone with a Bluetooth printer.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the printer screen">
    On the phone, go to **Home → Devices → Receipt printer**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick this phone's stations">
    Under **Prep stations**, switch on the stations this phone prints. If another phone already prints one, the app asks before moving it here.
  </Step>
</Steps>

One phone can hold several stations. With one printer for Bar and Kitchen, switch both on and you get one slip per station, with a gap to tear between them.

## Paper settings for printers without a cutter

Under **Paper** on the same screen:

- **Feed lines before tear**: how much blank paper follows each slip (2, 4, 6 or 8; 4 is the default). Raise it if slips are hard to tear apart.
- **One item per kitchen ticket**: prints a separate slip for each item, handy when each drink goes on its own cup.

These settings belong to the phone, so they apply to every slip it prints.

## Ticket status and Retry

**Settings → Printing** shows each station's status:

- **waiting**: tickets queued for that station. They print as soon as its phone picks them up.
- **failed**: tickets that didn't reach the printer. Check the printer, then tap **Retry** to send them again.
- **no phone**: no phone prints this station yet. Its tickets wait until you claim it on a phone.

If one station's printer is off, the other stations still print as normal.
