**Outcome:** you can identify the missing input without recording the same event twice.

Open **Performance → Monthly review**, then **Review missing information**. Issues are grouped by sales evidence, historical menu costs, expenses, and monthly review.

Record links open in a new tab so your chosen month and unsaved review stay in place. After saving a correction, return to Monthly review and reload before reviewing the month again. A payment warning is separate from an uncompleted order or missing ingredient cost.

## Order warnings

| Message | Next action |
| --- | --- |
| Order not completed yet | Check what happened. Complete the online order only if the customer received it |
| Payment not verified | Open the order and resolve its payment status; completion does not verify payment |
| Financial history is pending reconciliation | Open order history and compare recorded events with what actually happened |
| Historical cost inputs or selection identities are missing | Check the product’s costing and historical records; a new mapping may not repair an old order |

## Unknown ingredient consumption

Unknown does not mean RM 0. Check that the product, selected options, recipe quantities, and ingredient costs are configured.

If the order predates reliable cost or option records, retain the incomplete state rather than inventing a historical cost. Correct factual evidence where you can establish what happened. Contact support if the missing historical record cannot be resolved through the available forms.

## Expense and review warnings

1. Confirm draft invoices that belong to the month.
2. Add missing estimates or actual costs in their service months.
3. Review category coverage and fixed or variable treatment.
4. Reconfirm the monthly review after inputs change.

Do not confirm that sales are complete while another sales channel is missing.

## After a correction

Return to Overview for the answer, then Monthly review to confirm the changed inputs. A previous email is a saved snapshot; later corrections can change the current dashboard.

**Ready when:** warnings are resolved or the remaining unknowns are explicitly understood. Keep an incomplete answer incomplete until its inputs are supported.
