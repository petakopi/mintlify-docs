---
title: "4. Record sales events"
description: "Keep payment, preparation, and handover clear."
---

**Outcome:** Performance can tell what was sold, what was made, and when it happened.

Before starting, [configure menu costs](/manage-shop/performance/costing). Open **Performance → Sales activity** for the selected month’s order, refund, and consumption evidence. Open **Online Store → Orders** to handle web orders.

## Follow the order

1. Verify payment through the order’s payment workflow.
2. Use **Start Preparing** when staff start, then mark the order ready when preparation is finished.
3. Complete the order when the whole order is handed to the customer.
4. Review **Performance evidence** if financial information is missing.

Payment and handover answer different questions. Payment records money received; handover recognizes the sale for Performance. Payment alone does not prove the customer received their order.

## Choose an evidence event

| Event | What you are recording |
| --- | --- |
| Handover | The whole order was supplied to the customer |
| Preparation | Staff started making the order |
| Cancellation | The order was cancelled; specify made, not made, or unknown |
| Allowance | A documented reduction in the sale value |

Enter when the event actually happened in Malaysia time and explain your evidence. Do not add another handover just because an ingredient-cost warning remains.

Evidence entries affect reporting. They do not collect money, issue a refund, or change the operational order status. Use the payment workflow for payment or refund actions.

## Cancellations and refunds

If cancelled goods were made, their ingredient consumption remains a cost. If you do not know whether they were made, choose unknown rather than treating the cost as zero.

For an order handed over in September and refunded in October, the revenue reduction belongs to October. Original ingredient costs remain with September’s sale.

## Corrections

Open **Performance evidence**, find the incorrect entry, and choose **Correct this entry**. Record the accurate event details and reason. The old entry remains visible as replaced history.

A handover can be recorded while payment remains unverified or cost remains incomplete. Resolve each warning separately using [missing information](/manage-shop/performance/missing-information).

**Ready when:** supplied orders have handover evidence, payment is verified, and costs are complete or clearly flagged.
