---
title: "4. Record sales events"
description: "Keep payment, preparation, and completion clear."
---

**Outcome:** Performance can tell what was sold, what was made, and when it happened.

Before starting, [configure menu costs](/manage-shop/performance/costing). Open **Performance → Sales activity** for the selected month’s order, refund, and consumption evidence. Open **Weekly activity** for one Monday-to-Monday week. Open **Online Store → Orders** to handle web orders.

## Follow the order

1. Verify payment through the order’s payment workflow.
2. Use **Start Preparing** when staff start, then mark the order ready when preparation is finished.
3. Complete an online order when the whole order is handed to the customer.
4. Review **Performance evidence** if financial information is missing.

A POS sale counts when it is paid, because the customer is served at the counter. An online order counts when it is completed, because payment alone does not prove the customer received it. Completing a POS sale in Orders does not move its sale date.

## Choose an evidence event

| Event | What you are recording |
| --- | --- |
| Sold | The whole order reached the customer |
| Preparation | Staff started making the order |
| Cancellation | The order was cancelled; specify made, not made, or unknown |
| Allowance | A documented reduction in the sale value |

Enter when the event actually happened in Malaysia time and explain your evidence. Do not add another sold entry just because an ingredient-cost warning remains.

Evidence entries affect reporting. They do not collect money, issue a refund, or change the operational order status. Use the payment workflow for payment or refund actions.

## Cancellations and refunds

If cancelled goods were made, their ingredient consumption remains a cost. If you do not know whether they were made, choose unknown rather than treating the cost as zero.

For an order sold in September and refunded in October, the revenue reduction belongs to October. Original ingredient costs remain with September’s sale.

## Corrections

Open **Performance evidence**, find the incorrect entry, and choose **Correct this entry**. Record the accurate event details and reason. The old entry remains visible as replaced history.

A sale can be recorded while payment remains unverified or cost remains incomplete. Resolve each warning separately using [missing information](/manage-shop/performance/missing-information).

**Ready when:** online orders are completed, payment is verified, and costs are complete or clearly flagged.
