title: "Sales by label"
description: "See what your labelled products sold this month, such as a vendor's goods."
---

**Outcome:** at month end you know what each vendor's products sold, so you can work out what you owe them.

Open **Performance → Sales by label**. Use the ‹ › buttons to move between months. You need a Daily report time first; if you have not chosen one, the page links to Performance settings.

Labels are private: customers never see them. Add one on a product's form, or select several products in **Products** and choose **Label…**.

Each row is one label with its **Units** and **Sales**. When a refund happened, the table shows **Net sales** and a **Refunds** column. **No label** collects everything else.

Example: Kak Ani bakes your croissants and keeps 70% of what they sell for. Label them **Kak Ani**. At month end the **Kak Ani** row shows 200 units and RM 1,000.00, so you owe her RM 700.00.

## How labels are counted

- A product with two labels counts under both, so rows can add up to more than the month's sales.
- Only orders through petakopi.my count. Keyed-in daily totals have no products, so they are not here.
- For a period that is not a whole month, use the label chips on [Menu Pricing](/manage-shop/performance/menu-pricing).
