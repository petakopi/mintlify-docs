---
title: "Sales by item"
description: "See what each item sold this month, what it cost you and what it left."
---

**Outcome:** you know which items earn the most and which earn the least, after ingredients.

Open **Performance → Sales by item**. Pick **Day**, **Week** or **Month**, then use the ‹ › buttons to move to the previous or next one. You need a Daily report time first; if you have not chosen one, the page links to Performance settings.

## The cards

Four cards total the period:

- **Units sold**: how many items sold.
- **Sales**: what those items sold for.
- **Cost**: their ingredient cost.
- **Gross profit**: Sales minus Cost, with the gross margin underneath.

Under each card, a line compares it with the previous day, week or month, for example “▲ 12% vs last month · RM 520.00”. While the period is still open, it compares up to the same point, for example “vs same time last month”. If nothing sold by then, it reads “No sales by this time last month”.

Example: on 17 November, Sales of RM 5,660.80 is compared with sales from 1 October up to the same time on 17 October, not with all of October.

When some items have no cost, a line under the cards reads, for example, “Cost and gross profit are on RM 5,563.00 of sales with a cost. 7 items have no cost yet.” **Cost** and **Gross profit** then cover only the costed items, so the gross margin is worked out on that RM 5,563.00, not on all sales.

## The table

The page lists every item sold in the period, with **Sold**, **Sales**, **Cost**, **Gross profit** and **Gross margin**. Sort by any of them: tap a column heading on a wide screen, or pick a sort on your phone.

Example: you sold 120 lattes at RM 10.00. The row shows Sales RM 1,200.00 and Cost RM 360.00 (RM 3.00 a cup), so Gross profit is RM 840.00 and Gross margin 70%. Sort by **Gross margin**, lowest first, to see which items to reprice.

## How items are counted

- Only orders through petakopi.my count. Keyed-in daily totals have no items, so they are not here.
- Refunded and cancelled orders are left out.
- Sales are item prices before order discounts and vouchers, so they can add up to more than **Sales** on [Sales summary](/manage-shop/performance/sales).
- Cost is the ingredient cost of what sold. Gross profit here is before rent, wages and bills.
- An item without a cost shows no cost, gross profit or gross margin. [Cost it](/manage-shop/performance/costing) to fill them in.
- **Cost** here can differ from **Ingredient cost (est.)** on [Sales summary](/manage-shop/performance/sales). This page counts each costed item, even in an order that has an uncosted open item. Sales summary leaves out such an order entirely, and adds waste and reward drinks at cost.
