description: "See what each item sold this month, what it cost you and what it left."
---

**Outcome:** you know which items earn the most and which earn the least, after ingredients.

Open **Performance → Sales by item**. Use the ‹ › buttons to move between months. You need a Daily report time first; if you have not chosen one, the page links to Performance settings.

The page lists every item sold in the period, with **Sold**, **Sales**, **Cost**, **Profit** and **Margin**. Sort by any of them: tap a column heading on a wide screen, or pick a sort on your phone.

Example: you sold 120 lattes at RM 10.00. The row shows Sales RM 1,200.00 and Cost RM 360.00 (RM 3.00 a cup), so Profit is RM 840.00 and Margin 70%. Sort by **Margin**, lowest first, to see which items to reprice.

## How items are counted

- Only orders through petakopi.my count. Keyed-in daily totals have no items, so they are not here.
- Refunded and cancelled orders are left out.
- Sales are item prices before order discounts and vouchers, so they can add up to more than **Net sales** on [Sales summary](/manage-shop/performance/sales).
- Cost is the ingredient cost of what sold. Profit here is before rent, wages and bills.
- An item without a cost shows no cost, profit or margin. [Cost it](/manage-shop/performance/costing) to fill them in.
