---
title: "Older expenses"
description: "Read previously logged expenses and their receipt attachments"
---

Open **Performance → Expenses** and choose **Older expenses (before Performance)** at the bottom of the page to inspect expenses recorded through the older expense workflow. This page is read-only; the mobile expense workflow remains available.

The page shows one calendar month at a time, in Malaysia time. Use **Previous month** and **Next month** to move between months. Each entry shows its date, description, category, amount, notes, and receipt attachments when available. When a month has more entries than fit on one page, use **Previous page** and **Next page**.

These are owner-entered expenses, separate from [Loyverse transactions](/manage-shop/loyverse-transactions) and [Performance expenses](/manage-shop/performance/expenses). Opening this page does not move or count them in Performance. Avoid entering the same bill again without reviewing its service month and whether it is already represented there.
