Open **Performance → Overview** to answer **“Has my coffee shop covered its costs this month?”** The compact page combines recorded sales, estimated ingredients consumed, operating expenses, order count, average order value, and one attention summary.

This guide follows **web orders**. POS setup is outside this guide. Performance does not use Loyverse sales; [Pulse](/manage-shop/pulse/overview) remains a separate feature.

## Set up your inputs

1. [Create your menu](/manage-shop/performance/menu): products, prices, and customer choices.
2. [Assign costs](/manage-shop/performance/costing): ingredients, recipes, and option portions.
3. [Record expenses](/manage-shop/performance/expenses): estimates and reviewed invoices.

## Get your answer

1. Open **Reports and settings** to [choose the reporting time](/manage-shop/performance/monthly-answer#reporting-time).
2. Open **Sales activity** to [trace orders, refunds, and consumption](/manage-shop/performance/sales).
3. Open **Monthly review** to [check costs and confirm inputs](/manage-shop/performance/monthly-answer#review-inputs).
4. Return to **Overview** for the answer and [resolve missing information](/manage-shop/performance/missing-information).
5. Use **Reports and settings** to [set up email reports](/manage-shop/performance/email-reports) when sending is available.

Open **Weekly activity** when you want the current week’s sales, refunds, and daily orders. That page does not answer whether the month’s costs were covered.

## What the answer means

**Net sales − estimated ingredients consumed − operating costs = estimated operating surplus or shortfall.**

This is not your bank balance or final net profit. Missing costs or sales can prevent a complete answer. Estimates remain estimates even after you confirm that you reviewed them.

<Note>If you also sell outside the web ordering system, do not confirm that all shop sales are captured unless those sales are included. A partial sales record cannot answer the whole-shop question.</Note>
