Start with **“Has my coffee shop covered its costs this month?”** Performance combines recorded sales, estimated ingredients consumed, and operating expenses to answer that question.

This guide follows **web orders**. POS setup is outside this guide. Performance does not use Loyverse sales; [Pulse](/manage-shop/pulse/overview) remains a separate feature.

## Set up your inputs

1. [Create your menu](/manage-shop/performance/menu): products, prices, and customer choices.
2. [Assign costs](/manage-shop/performance/costing): ingredients, recipes, and option portions.
3. [Record expenses](/manage-shop/performance/expenses): estimates and reviewed invoices.

## Get your answer

1. [Record what happened to orders](/manage-shop/performance/sales): payment, preparation, and handover.
2. [Review the month](/manage-shop/performance/monthly-answer): reporting time, coverage, and costs.
3. [Resolve missing information](/manage-shop/performance/missing-information) before relying on the result.
4. [Set up email reports](/manage-shop/performance/email-reports) when sending is available.

## What the answer means

**Net sales − estimated ingredients consumed − operating costs = estimated operating surplus or shortfall.**

This is not your bank balance or final net profit. Missing costs or sales can prevent a complete answer. Estimates remain estimates even after you confirm that you reviewed them.

<Note>If you also sell outside the web ordering system, do not confirm that all shop sales are captured unless those sales are included. A partial sales record cannot answer the whole-shop question.</Note>
