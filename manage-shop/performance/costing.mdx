---
title: "2. Assign ingredient costs"
description: "Link products and options to recipes or bought-in costs."
---

**Outcome:** each menu selection has an explainable ingredient cost.

Before starting, [save your menu](/manage-shop/performance/menu). Use **Costs & Pricing → Items** for ingredient purchase units and **Recipes** for quantities used per serving. The [Items guide](/manage-shop/costs-and-pricing/items) and [Recipes guide](/manage-shop/costs-and-pricing/recipes) cover those forms.

## Open product costing

Go to **Online Store → Products** and edit your saved product. Find the **Costing** section and click **Set up or review product costing**.

On that page, **Base cost** links the product to its recipe or bought-in unit cost. Creating a recipe alone does not link it to a product.

## Set the base cost

1. Enter each ingredient’s pack price, pack size, and usable yield in Items.
2. Create a recipe using the amount consumed per serving.
3. Return to the product’s **Costing** page and find **Base cost**.
4. Under **How is this product costed?**, choose the costing method and select the recipe or enter the bought-in unit cost.
5. Save costing to link those costs to the product.

For our fictional latte, beans cost RM 1.44, milk RM 1.26, and a cup RM 0.30: **RM 3 per serving**. Selling it for RM 10 leaves RM 7 before other costs.

Confirm zero only when the ingredient cost really is zero. **Not configured** is missing information, not a free product.

## Option costs

Review every offered option and choose its effect:

| Choice | Use it when |
| --- | --- |
| Replacement recipe/cost | The choice changes the complete drink, such as oat milk |
| Adds ingredients | The choice adds a portion, such as an extra shot |
| No cost change | You have checked that the choice changes no ingredient cost |
| Needs review | You have not decided yet; costing remains incomplete |

For multiple replacement choices, map the full combination to one complete recipe. Avoid adding both the original and replacement recipe together.

### Replacement or added ingredient?

Use a **replacement recipe/cost** when the base recipe already contains the ingredient being replaced. Use **Adds ingredients** when the base recipe leaves that part out and the option supplies it.

For example, if the Latte recipe already contains espresso, dairy milk, and a cup:

| Option | Costing choice |
| --- | --- |
| Dairy milk | No cost change |
| Oat milk | Replacement recipe/cost → Oat Latte recipe |
| Soy milk | Replacement recipe/cost → Soy Latte recipe |

Each replacement recipe must contain the complete drink cost. Do not add oat milk on top of a recipe that already includes dairy milk, or the calculation will count both milks.

You can use **Adds ingredients** instead when the base Latte recipe contains only espresso and the cup:

| Option | Costing choice |
| --- | --- |
| Dairy milk | Adds ingredients → Dairy milk portion |
| Oat milk | Adds ingredients → Oat milk portion |
| Soy milk | Adds ingredients → Soy milk portion |

For an Americano, a House Blend base recipe can use **No cost change** for House Blend. If Single Origin uses different beans, map it to a complete **Single Origin Americano** replacement recipe.

## Shared portions

You can reuse a shared extra portion or customize it for one product. Sharing a menu option group alone does not establish portion size.

Before confirming an edit to a shared portion, review the affected products. Their future costs may change too.

## Preview selections

1. Select a combination customers can order.
2. Check the base or replacement cost and any extras.
3. Repeat for the other offered combinations.
4. Save your costing changes.

A successful preview proves that selection only. It does not prove the whole menu is complete.

## Historical costs

Performance uses the cost at preparation time. If preparation was not recorded, it uses a labelled handover-time estimate. Later price edits do not rewrite recorded past costs.

Adding a mapping today may not fix older orders with missing selection identities or historical inputs. Use [missing-information guidance](/manage-shop/performance/missing-information).

**Ready when:** your offered combinations preview with complete costs and the configuration is saved.
