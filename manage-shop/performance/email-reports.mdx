**Outcome:** owners can follow Performance without rebuilding the monthly picture from an email.

Before starting, choose a [Daily report time](/manage-shop/performance/monthly-answer#reporting-time) and review the dashboard. Open **Performance → Email reports**.

<Note>Sending must be enabled for the feature before emails can be delivered. Saving preferences does not prove delivery is available. Check the availability and delivery status shown in the app.</Note>

## Recipients and frequency

1. Choose the current owners who should receive reports.
2. Opt each recipient into daily, weekly, or monthly reports as needed.
3. Save the preferences.
4. Check report and delivery history after a period finishes.

Subscriptions start off. Reports use your reporting cutoff: daily periods, Monday-to-Sunday weeks, and reporting months. An hourly check finds due reports; delivery is not guaranteed at the exact cutoff minute.

## Read a snapshot

An email describes the information available when that report was generated. **No recorded sales** does not prove the shop was closed or that actual sales were zero.

Open the dashboard to investigate missing costs or sales. Later corrections can change the dashboard while the original report remains unchanged. Correction emails are not sent automatically.

## Delivery status

Inspect the report’s delivery status if an email is missing. Check the recipient inbox before retrying an uncertain delivery; the provider may already have accepted it. Dismissing a delivery issue does not mark the email as sent.

<Accordion title="What happens after enabling reports or an outage?">
The next completed period can include activity from before you opted in. After an outage, the system catches up with the latest missed summary for each frequency rather than emailing every missed period.
</Accordion>

**Ready when:** the intended owners and frequencies are saved, and delivery status confirms what happened.
