title: "Review a week of activity"
description: "See this week's recorded sales, refunds, and orders."
---

**Outcome:** you can read one week of recorded activity and open a day, without treating that week as the monthly cost-coverage answer.

Open **Performance → Weekly activity**.

## Read the week

1. The line under the title shows the week and its dates; hover it for exact times. Use the ‹ › buttons by the week dates to move between weeks; **This week** returns to the current one. The address keeps the selected week when you open an order’s evidence.
2. A current week says **Week so far**. Later days are not confirmed zero sales.
3. Days with no orders or refunds are hidden behind **N days with no recorded activity · Show**. Select **Show** to list them in date order; each says **No recorded activity**. That does not mean the shop was closed. If no day has activity, the page shows **No sales this week** instead of the list. An empty week does not confirm the shop was closed.
4. Net sales and orders lead the week; supplied sales, refunds, ingredient cost (est.), and the average follow on one line. Orders that need attention are grouped by cause at the top, each with a button to fix it. Open a day to see its orders. The weekly average uses the week’s orders. It is not an average of the daily averages.
5. When a week crosses two months, the top line links each month; open each monthly answer on its own. Rent and wages stay on the monthly answer.

Refunds reduce the week in which they are recorded. They do not remove the original order or its ingredient cost from the earlier week.
