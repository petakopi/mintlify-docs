---
title: "Bought vs Used"
description: "Compare what your invoices say you bought with what your sales and waste log say you used."
---

**Outcome:** see, per material, how much of what you bought your recipes and waste log can account for over the last 3 complete months.

Open **Performance → Bought vs Used**. You need a Daily report time and confirmed ingredient invoices under **Expenses**. A new shop sees "Come back after your first full month" until one month has closed.

## Match your invoice lines

Invoice lines are written by suppliers, so the page needs to know which material each one is. You match each line once; every confirmed invoice with the same description and unit counts from then on, past and future.

1. Under **Purchases not matched**, find a line, such as "Fresh Milk 1L · ctn · 3 lines · RM 450.00".
2. Choose **Match** and pick the material, such as **Fresh milk**. Search matches any part of the name.
3. Check how much one invoice unit holds, such as **1 ctn = 12000 ml**. The box is filled in for you: 1 kg = 1000 g, 1 L = 1000 ml, and anything else (ctn, pack, box) starts at the material's pack size.
4. Choose **Save match**.

For a line that is not a material, such as bought-in croissants or cleaning cloths, choose **Not a material**. It leaves the unmatched total.

The unit is part of the match. If a supplier writes "carton" instead of "ctn", that line shows as unmatched again, so it never counts with the wrong amount. Open **Matched** to edit or remove a match; the page updates on the next load.

In the invoice editor, each purchase line says what it counts as, such as "Counts as 24 L Fresh milk.", or links back here when it is not matched.

## Read the comparison

Each material shows four figures for the period:

| Figure | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| Bought | Matched lines on confirmed invoices, in the material's unit |
| Used in sales | Recipe amounts for every sale with a cost, after yield |
| Logged waste | What you logged on the [Waste](/manage-shop/performance/waste) page |
| Unexplained | Bought minus used minus waste, in units, RM and % of bought |

Example: you bought 60 L of milk; sales recipes account for 52 L and the waste log for 1 L. Unexplained is 7 L. At an average of RM 7.50 per litre, that is RM 52.50.

Materials are sorted by Unexplained RM, largest first. **By month** shows each month on its own; a big buy near a month end makes single months swing, so read the 3-month figure first.

**Used more than bought** means the figures go the other way: stock bought before this period, purchases not matched yet, or recipe amounts set higher than staff pour.

## How much of your sales has a cost

Usage only comes from sales with a cost. The page checks what share of your sales (in RM) has one:

- **70 % and over:** the comparison shows as is.
- **50 % to under 70 %:** it shows with a **Rough** badge.
- **Under 50 %:** usage is hidden and only what you bought shows. Cost more of your menu under **Products**.

Keyed-in daily totals have no items, so they count as sales without a cost.

## What to check when Unexplained is big

Check, in this order:

1. Is the match right (1 ctn = 12000 ml)?
2. Do your recipes match what staff pour (a 200 ml latte poured at 240 ml)?
3. Is waste logged every day?
4. Is stock still on the shelf from a big buy?

Free drinks with no sale count here too: log them as **Staff drink** or **Given free** on the Waste page.

## Limits

- There is no opening or closing stock, so building up or running down stock shows as a gap.
- Unmatched purchases are left out of Bought; the unmatched total says how much.
- Bought-in products costed by unit cost are left out.
