---
title: "Sales by payment method"
description: "See how your counter customers paid this month."
---

**Outcome:** you know how much came in as cash, QR, TNG and card, so you can match it with your drawer and bank.

Open **Performance → Sales by Payment** (the page is titled **Sales by payment method**). Use the ‹ › buttons to move between months. You need a Daily report time first; if you have not chosen one, the page links to Performance settings.

Each row is one payment method: **Cash**, **QR Payment (Manual)**, **TNG** or **Card**. It shows the income after refunds, the number of orders and its share of the total, with any refunds in red. Online orders are one row of their own.

Example: this month the counter took RM 2,500.00 in cash from 200 orders and RM 2,500.00 by QR from 180 orders. The Cash row shows RM 2,500.00 and 50%; so does the QR row. Compare the Cash figure with what you banked from the drawer.

## What is not split

- Keyed-in daily totals have no payment method, so they are not here.
- Methods come from POS orders. A month with no counter sales shows "No counter sales this month".
