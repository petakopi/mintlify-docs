title: "Loyverse transactions"
description: "Inspect imported backup receipts separately from Performance"
---

Open **Loyverse → Backup history** in the Manage sidebar to inspect the read-only receipt copy held by petakopi. The sidebar shows it only while Loyverse is connected. Loyverse is a backup integration; its transactions are excluded from [Performance](/manage-shop/performance/overview).

## Find a receipt

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose dates">
    The list shows its date range above the receipts. Click **Change dates**, set **From date** and **To date**, then click **Apply dates**. Dates use the Malaysia calendar, with a maximum range of 366 days.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the receipt number">
    The list shows transaction time, sale or refund type, currency, imported amount, and cancellation state. When there is more than one page, use **Previous page** or **Next page**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Inspect its stored details">
    The detail page shows line items, modifiers, payments, and the original receipt for a refund when that original is in the backup. Missing payload fields are labelled as unavailable.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Return to your list">
    **Back to Loyverse transactions** keeps your selected dates and page.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Understand the backup

Cancelled receipts retain their imported amount and are marked **Cancelled**. Refunds are labelled separately. This page does not calculate profit or combine different currencies into a headline total.

The line under the title shows whether Loyverse is connected, the last successful scheduled receipt poll (or **never**), and a link to Settings. Open **About this backup** for how imports work. The backup does not promise that every transaction in Loyverse has been imported. An empty list means no copied receipts were found in those dates; it does not confirm that the external shop had no activity.

## After disconnecting

Previously imported receipts remain readable. New receipts stop importing until you reconnect. Connection and backfill live on **Loyverse → Settings**; disconnect and data-wipe are in its **⋯** menu. See [Loyverse](/manage-shop/loyverse).

**Wipe all Loyverse data** deletes the local backup. Disconnecting and wiping are different actions.

## After Pulse retires

Old Pulse links open a transition page with separate destinations for first-party Performance, Loyverse transactions, and legacy expenses. Valid old date selections carry into the history links as Malaysia calendar dates. Performance uses its own reporting cutoff and different financial definitions; Pulse's “True net” and burn chart have not moved into it.

Pulse daily recaps and weekly/monthly verdict emails stop at cutover. Loyverse receipt importing continues while connected.
